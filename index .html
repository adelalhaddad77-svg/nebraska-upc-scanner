NEBRASKA UPC SCANNER · iOS APP

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Effective Date: April 11, 2026

01 Overview

✓ Nebraska UPC Scanner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Nebraska UPC Scanner is a barcode scanning app designed to validate UPC codes against a locally stored product list. The app is built with privacy by design — everything runs on your device with no external servers involved.

02 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Camera images or photos
  • Scanned barcode data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Payment or financial information

03 Camera Permission

The app requires access to your iPhone camera solely to scan UPC barcodes. This is the core function of the app.

✓ Camera images are processed entirely on your device and are never stored, saved, or transmitted anywhere.

You can revoke camera access at any time in Settings → Nebraska UPC Scanner → Camera.

04 Local Data Storage

All data used by the app is stored locally on your device only:

  • The UPC product list is bundled within the app itself
  • Scan history is stored only in your device memory and cleared when the app closes
  • No data is written to iCloud, external servers, or any third-party service

05 No Internet Connection Required

Nebraska UPC Scanner works completely offline. The app does not make any network requests and does not communicate with any external servers or APIs.

✓ The app functions with no internet connection and sends zero data over any network.

06 Third-Party Services

Nebraska UPC Scanner does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. We do not use:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase
  • Facebook SDK or any social media SDKs
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting tools

07 Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is designed for business and retail use.

08 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
